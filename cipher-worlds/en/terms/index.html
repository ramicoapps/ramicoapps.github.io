Consumers in Israel: the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981, section 14C, gives consumers a right to cancel a "distance sale" (a sale made without the seller and the buyer being together, e.g. online) – in general within 14 days, as set out in the law. The law also lists exceptions, which include "information" as defined in the Computers Law, 5755-1995, and goods that can be recorded, reproduced or copied once the consumer has opened their original packaging. When a consumer cancels for a reason other than a defect or a breach, the law allows a cancellation fee of up to 5% of the price or NIS 100, whichever is lower (section 14E).
