Privacy Policy

Updated 8.11.2026

Version 1.0 · Effective date: 8.11.2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Cipher Worlds: Morse (the "game") on Android or iOS, who handles it, and what choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain language. If anything is unclear, write to us.

Children and parents: please also read our Children's Privacy & Information for Parents.

1. Who we are

The game is made and published by Rami Cohen, an exempt dealer (osek patur) in Israel, under the trade name RamiCoApps ("we", "us").

  • E-mail: ramicoapps@gmail.com (privacy questions, requests and support)

2. The short version

  • The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, address, location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • We have no server of our own and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. Your progress is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some information from your device to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud (section 5). This is the main way information leaves your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, not by us (section 6).
  • On Android you can choose to save your progress to Google Play Games (section 7), and you can export a backup file yourself (section 8).
  • When the game first starts it asks for your year of birth only to choose the right kind of ads. It keeps only an age group, never the year (section 4).

3. What the game stores on your device

The game keeps a local database on your device only. It contains:

  • game progress: levels, stars, XP, achievements, missions, streak and learning statistics;
  • settings: language, sound, speed, vibration, tap calibration and similar choices;
  • your virtual wallet (coins, diamonds, hints), items you own, your Pip's World and story progress;
  • a record of store purchases: the product, its state and the transaction id the store returns (so that Premium and packs work and can be restored);
  • an anonymous local profile (a random id created on the device, with no name or contact details);
  • your age group (section 4).

We cannot see or access this data. It is not sent to us.

What the game never collects

No name, e-mail, phone number, postal address, date of birth, precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The game has no chat, no user-generated content, no sharing between players and no friends lists (Pip's friends are story characters).

4. The age screen

On first launch, before any ad is requested, the game asks "What year were you born?" There is no pre-selected year and no reward for any answer. The game converts the answer into one of three age groups – under 13, 13–15, or 16 or older – and stores only the group on the device. The year itself is not stored or sent anywhere.

The age group is used only to choose ad settings (section 5). It can be changed only by Settings → Reset everything, which asks the question again. If no answer is given, the game does not request ads.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows an ad between some levels (never with Premium) and optional "rewarded" ads that you choose to watch for a hint, a chest or extra tries.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google. When an ad is requested, Google's software in the game collects information directly from your device. According to Google's own documentation, this can include:

  • your IP address (which can be used to estimate general location);
  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID);
  • interactions with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and video views);
  • diagnostic and device information (such as app performance data).

Google uses this information for advertising, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, and processes it under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Ads depend on the age group

Age groupHow ads are requested
Under 13Marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, content rated G, non-personalized ads only.
13–15Marked as under the age of consent, content rated G, non-personalized ads only.
16 or olderContent rated G. Personalized ads only where Google's consent tools allow it (see below); otherwise non-personalized.

"Non-personalized" ads are not based on your past behaviour. They are chosen by context (for example the app and your general location), but Google may still collect the information listed above for measurement and fraud prevention and to limit how often you see an ad.

Consent and privacy options

Where Google requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads start. When Google indicates it is needed, a Privacy options button in Settings lets you open that form again and change your choice.

Your ad controls

  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Google: you can manage ad personalization in Google's ad settings (My Ad Center) – see Google's page on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • iOS: the game does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you across other companies' apps. It therefore does not get access to the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Premium removes the ads between levels. Rewarded ads remain optional.

6. Purchases

All purchases (Premium and packs of hints, coins or diamonds) are made only through Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase. The store processes your payment details, store account and purchase history under its own privacy policy. We never see your card or payment details. The game receives only what the store returns to confirm the purchase (product, state and transaction id), and keeps it on your device.

7. Google Play Games saved games (Android, optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games from the game, a copy of your progress is saved in your own Play Games account so you can restore it on another device. The copy has the same content as a backup file (section 8): progress, changed settings (including the age group) and the anonymous profile. It never contains purchases or Premium. This data is stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The game does not read your Google name or e-mail address. If you do not sign in, nothing is saved there. This option is not offered to players under 13.

8. Backup files you export

In Settings you can export a backup file. The file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send or save it (for example your own cloud storage or e-mail). That service handles the file under its own terms. A backup never contains purchases or Premium.

10. Who receives information

  • Google (AdMob ads; Google Play purchases; Play Games saves if you sign in).
  • Apple (App Store purchases on iOS).
  • Whoever you choose to send a backup file to.
  • Authorities, only if required by law (we hold almost no data that could be disclosed).

11. International transfers

We are in Israel. Google and Apple operate worldwide. Google states that information may be processed on servers located outside the country where you live, and that it applies the same protections and relies on legal frameworks for international transfers (see the Google Privacy Policy). Apple describes its transfers in its own privacy policy.

12. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you reset it in the game (section 13) or uninstall the game.
  • Google and Apple: for the periods set out in their own policies. Google states that retention depends on the type of data and your settings, and that some advertising data in server logs is deleted or anonymized after a set period.
  • Us: if you e-mail us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer you and to keep a record of the request.

13. Deleting your information

  • In the game – Settings → Reset:
    • _Reset progress_ deletes your progress;
    • _Reset settings_ deletes your settings;
    • _Reset everything_ deletes all progress and settings (including the age group) and starts the game from the beginning. The record of store purchases is kept so Premium can still work and be restored.
  • Uninstall the game to delete the whole local database from your device, including the purchase record and the anonymous profile.
  • Google Play Games: you can delete the game's saved data, or your Play Games profile, in the Play Games settings or at play.google.com/games/profile – see Delete your Play Games data.
  • Google advertising data: use your device's advertising ID settings and Google's ad and activity controls described in Google's privacy policy. We cannot delete data held by Google.
  • Apple: use Apple's privacy tools for your Apple Account.

Because we do not have a copy of your game data, we cannot delete it for you; the steps above are the way to do it.

14. Your rights

Israel. Under the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (as amended, including Amendment 13 in force since 14 August 2025), you may ask to access personal data about you held in a database, and ask to correct or delete data that is not correct, complete, clear or up to date.

EEA and UK. Under the GDPR / UK GDPR you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, to data portability (the backup file lets you take your data with you), and to withdraw consent at any time (for ads, through Privacy options).

How to use your rights. Write to ramicoapps@gmail.com. Please note that we do not hold your game data on any server and have no account linked to you, so in most cases we will have nothing to show, correct or delete; we will tell you that and explain how to reach the data held by Google or Apple. We may ask for information needed to handle a request, but never more than necessary.

Complaints. You may complain to a data protection authority:

  • Israel: the Privacy Protection Authority (Ministry of Justice);
  • EEA: the data protection authority in your country (list on the EDPB website);
  • UK: the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

15. Children

The game is for a mixed audience, including children. It does not ask children for personal information, has no chat and no accounts, and shows players under 16 only non-personalized, G-rated ads (players under 13 are also marked as child-directed). Details, and how parents can control purchases, are in Children's Privacy & Information for Parents.

16. Security

Your game data stays on your device, protected by your device's own security. Information sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (TLS), according to Google. No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise that information will never be accessed without permission. Keep your device updated and protected with a screen lock, and store backup files only in places you trust.

17. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version with a new version number and effective date. If a change is significant, we will also tell players in the game or in the store listing before it takes effect.

18. Contact

RamiCoApps · ramicoapps@gmail.com
