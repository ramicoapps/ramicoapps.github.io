Privacy Policy

Updated 10.11.2026

1. About this policy

The game Cipher Worlds: Morse is operated by RamiCoApps, and we are responsible for this policy. It explains what information is kept and where, who receives information and why, and what your rights and choices are. It also has a section for parents (section 20).

The game teaches Morse code. It is distributed worldwide on Google Play and the App Store, and is made for a mixed audience: children (including under 13), teens and adults. Because children play it, we have designed it to collect as little as possible from everyone.

Questions? Write to us: ramicoapps@gmail.com.

2. Definitions

"You" or "the player": whoever plays the game. "We" or "us": RamiCoApps. "The game": Cipher Worlds: Morse for Android and iOS. "Personal information": information that identifies a person or makes it possible to identify them, including a device identifier. "Age group": one of the three groups set by the age screen (section 6).

3. The short version

  • The game has no accounts, no chat and no sharing between players. We have no server, and the game has no analytics or crash reporting of our own.
  • Your progress is stored only on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Before any ad, the game asks for your year of birth and keeps only an age group, never the year.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information from the device (section 7). Under 16, ads are never personalized.
  • Purchases go only through Google Play or the App Store. We never see payment details.

4. What is kept on your device

The game keeps a local database on your device only:

  • progress: levels, stars, XP, achievements, missions, streak and learning statistics;
  • settings: language, sound, speed, vibration, tap calibration and similar choices;
  • your virtual wallet (coins, diamonds, hints), items you own, your Pip's World and story progress;
  • a record of store purchases: the product, its state and the transaction id the store returns, so that Premium and packs work and can be restored;
  • an anonymous local profile: a random id created on the device, with no name or contact details;
  • your age group (section 6).

This information is not sent to us, and we have no access to it.

On Android the game is excluded from Google's automatic backup, and the data is not copied to your Google account. A direct transfer to a new phone (by cable or Wi-Fi) may still move it, between your phones and not through us. On iOS a device backup (iCloud or computer), if turned on, may include it. Google and Apple manage these settings; we have no access to them.

5. What the game does not collect, and how information is used

The game does not ask for or collect your name, e-mail, phone number, address, date of birth, precise location, contacts or photos, and does not use the camera or microphone. There is no chat, no messages, no user-generated content, no sharing between players and no real friends lists (Pip's friends are story characters). There are no contests with real prizes and no subscriptions.

The game's own code sends no information to any server. Information leaves the device only through the ad component (section 7), through the store when you buy something (section 9), through Google Play Games saves if you choose them (section 10), and in a backup file you export (section 11).

We do not sell personal information, and we do not build profiles of players.

6. The age screen

On first launch, before any ad is requested, the game asks "What year were you born?" with a list of years.

  • No year is pre-selected, the screen does not hint at any age limit, and no answer gives a reward or unlocks anything.
  • The game keeps only the age group on the device: under 13, 13–15, or 16 or older. The year of birth is not stored and not sent anywhere.
  • The age group is used only to choose ad settings (section 7) and to decide whether Play Games saves are offered (section 10).
  • Until the question is answered, the game does not request ads.
  • The age group is not included in backup files or Play Games saves, and restoring a backup never changes the device's age group.
  • It can be changed only with Settings → Reset everything, which deletes progress and asks again.

7. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows an ad between some levels (only if one is already ready, and never with Premium). There are also "rewarded" ads that you choose to watch for a hint, a chest or extra tries.

Ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google. When an ad is requested, Google's component in the game collects information directly from the device. According to Google, this can include:

  • your IP address (which can be used to estimate general location);
  • device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with ads and the game (for example launches, taps and video views);
  • diagnostic and device information (for example app performance).

Google uses this information to deliver ads, for measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy. It is sent to Google encrypted (TLS). We do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

Ads by age group

A "non-personalized" ad is not based on what you did before. It is chosen by context, for example the game and your general location. Even then, Google may collect the information above for measurement, fraud prevention and to limit how often you see the same ad.

8. Consent and choices

  • Google's consent form. Where Google requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), Google's consent form (UMP) is shown before ads start.
  • Offline. If the form cannot load (for example with no internet), the game requests no ad, unless a consent answer is already stored on the device. The next time an ad is asked for, the game tries again.
  • Changing your choice. Where the form is required, the Ad privacy options button on the Legal information screen (from Settings) opens the form again. The button appears only there.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings.
  • iOS: the game does not ask for Apple's tracking permission (ATT) and does not track you across other companies' apps, so it has no access to the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Google: you can manage ad personalization in My Ad Center, and read more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Premium removes the ads between levels. Rewarded ads remain your choice.

9. Purchases and mystery boxes

The game sells, only through Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase, a one-time Premium upgrade (no ads between levels, plus extra hints) and packs of hints, coins and diamonds. There are no subscriptions. The store processes your payment details, store account and purchase history under its own privacy policy. We never see card or payment details. The game receives from the store only the product, the purchase state and the transaction id, and keeps them on the device.

Coins and diamonds are virtual and have no cash value. Everything in the game can also be earned by playing.

The shop has mystery boxes: a random cosmetic item for coins or diamonds. The chance of each rarity is shown before a box is opened, and the items only change how things look, with no gameplay advantage. Since diamonds can be bought with money, parents may want to know about the boxes (section 20).

10. Google Play Games saves (Android, optional)

In Profile → Cloud save you can sign in to Google Play Games and save a copy of your progress in your own Play Games account, to restore it on another device.

  • It is offered only after the age question is answered with 13 or older. It is never offered under 13.
  • Nothing is saved there before you sign in.
  • The content is the same as a backup file (section 11), without the age group and without purchases or Premium.
  • The game does not read your Google name or e-mail address. Google stores the copy under its privacy policy.

11. Backup files

In Settings you can export a backup file and restore from it. The file contains your progress, the settings you changed, your wallet, items, Pip's World and the anonymous profile. It does not contain the age group, purchases or Premium.

The file is not encrypted, and anyone who receives it can read it. You choose where it goes (for example your own cloud storage or e-mail), and there it is subject to that service's terms. We have no control over the file once it is sent.

12. Contacting us

If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address, the content of your message and whatever you attached. Only this information reaches us directly. It is used only to answer and handle your request, never for mailings or advertising, and kept only as long as needed to handle and document it. You can ask for it to be deleted.

14. Who receives information

  • Google: AdMob ads, Google Play purchases, Play Games saves if you sign in, and messages to us (received in Gmail).
  • Apple: App Store purchases on iOS.
  • Whoever you choose to send a backup file to.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it and only to the extent needed (we hold almost no data that could be disclosed).

15. International transfers

We are in Israel. Google and Apple operate worldwide. Google states that information may be processed on servers outside the country where you live, that it applies the same protections, and that it relies on legal frameworks for international transfers (see the Google Privacy Policy). Apple describes its transfers in its own privacy policy.

16. Keeping and deleting information

How long: on your device – until you reset it in the game or uninstall it. At Google and Apple – under their own policies (Google states that this depends on the type of data and your settings, and that some advertising data in server logs is deleted or anonymized after a set period). With us – only e-mails (section 12).

How to delete:

  • Settings → Reset:
    • Reset progress deletes your progress;
    • Reset settings deletes your settings;
    • Reset everything deletes all progress and settings, including the age group, and starts again with the age question. The purchase record is kept so Premium still works and can be restored.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes the whole database from the device, including the purchase record and the anonymous profile.
  • Google Play Games: delete the game's saved data, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Play Games settings or at play.google.com/games/profile. See Delete your Play Games data.
  • Backup files and an iOS device backup are not deleted by this. Delete them separately, wherever they were saved.
  • Advertising data held by Google: reset or delete the advertising ID in the device settings, and use Google's own tools. We cannot delete data held by Google.
  • Apple: use Apple's privacy tools for your Apple Account.

We have no copy of your game data, so we cannot delete it for you or restore it.

17. Security

Your game data stays on the device, protected by the operating system's security, so it is worth locking and updating your device. The game adds no encryption of its own to the data or to backup files, so keep a backup only in a place you trust. The ad component's communication with Google is encrypted (TLS). No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise that information will never be accessed without permission.

18. Links to other websites

The legal documents, and links such as those in this policy (Google, Apple), open in the device's browser. The game sends no data to these sites, and we have no control over their content or their policies.

19. Your rights

Israel. Under the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (including Amendment 13, in force since 14 August 2025), you may ask to access personal data about you held in a database, and ask to correct or delete data that is not correct, complete, clear or up to date.

EEA and UK. Under the GDPR / UK GDPR you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, to data portability (the backup file lets you take your data with you), and to withdraw consent at any time (for ads, with Ad privacy options, section 8).

How to use them. Write to ramicoapps@gmail.com. Your game data is with you, not with us, and no account is linked to you, so in most cases we will have nothing to show, correct or delete. We will tell you so and explain how to do it in the game, or with Google and Apple. We may ask for information needed to handle a request, but never more than necessary.

Complaints. You may complain to a data protection authority: in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority (Ministry of Justice); in the EEA, the authority in your country (list on the EDPB website); in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO); in the US, about children, the Federal Trade Commission (FTC).

20. Children and teens

This section is mainly for parents and guardians.

What younger players get

  • Under 13: ads with Google's child treatment, G-rated and non-personalized (section 7). Play Games saves are not offered to them, and their progress stays on the device (backup files still work).
  • 13–15: ads with Google's teen treatment, G-rated and non-personalized.
  • Everyone: no account, chat, messages, public profile or sharing with other players. The game does not ask children for personal information, and does not ask them to consent to personalized ads.

Ads come from Google AdMob, which Google Play lists as a Families Self-Certified Ads SDK. Even when ads are non-personalized, Google receives some technical information (such as IP address and device information) for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Purchases, and how to prevent accidental ones

Children can buy in the game through the store, like any player (section 9). You may want to talk with your child about the mystery boxes and turn on the stores' own controls:

  • Google Play – purchase verification: in the Play Store settings, open "Purchase verification" and require it for all purchases. The options differ by region. See Purchase verification for Google Play.
  • Google Play – Family Link: for a managed child account, you can require your approval for purchases (all content, paid content, or in-app purchases only). See Purchase approvals on Google Play.
  • Apple – Ask to Buy: with Family Sharing, the organizer or a parent/guardian can require approval for a child's purchases. See Ask to Buy.
  • Apple – Screen Time: you can block in-app purchases in "Settings → Screen Time" (the exact menu depends on the iOS version). See Turn off in-app purchases with Screen Time.

Refunds are handled by the store that charged you (Google Play, or Apple's "Report a Problem"). Google Play, for example, accepts refund requests directly within a limited time after purchase – see Request a refund on Google Play. We cannot refund store charges ourselves, but we will help, for example if a purchase was not delivered. Details are in the Terms of Use.

Your child's information

The information is on your child's phone, not with us. You can write to ramicoapps@gmail.com to ask what information about your child we hold, or to ask us to correct or delete it. We normally hold none, and we will help you with the steps in section 16: reset everything, uninstall the game, delete the Play Games save (if your child signed in), delete backup files and reset the advertising ID.

Laws and store rules we take into account

We describe here how we approach these rules. This is not a statement that a regulator or store has reviewed or certified the game.

  • United States – COPPA. The Rule (16 CFR Part 312) protects children under 13. The FTC amended it in 2025 (published in the Federal Register on 22 April 2025), and the amendments include separate parental consent for disclosing children's information to third parties for targeted advertising. Our approach: the game does not ask children for personal information, it uses a neutral age screen, and under 13 ads are requested with the child treatment and non-personalized, so they are not targeted. We do not collect personal information from children ourselves.
  • European Union / UK – GDPR. Under Article 8, where an online service relies on consent, a child's consent is valid from age 16, and each country may set a lower age, not below 13. In the UK the age is 13. Because the age differs by country, the game treats every player under 16 as under the age of consent and shows them only non-personalized ads.
  • Israel – Protection of Privacy Law (including Amendment 13). The law has no specific children's-consent age. Parents or guardians may act for their children under the general law.
  • Google Play – Families Policy. For a mixed-audience app this includes a neutral age screen, ads for children only from Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs, no personalized or interest-based ads for children, and not transmitting the advertising ID and similar identifiers from children or users of unknown age.
  • Apple – App Store. The game is for a mixed audience and not only for children, so we do not list it in the App Store Kids Category. Apple's guideline 5.1.4 still requires apps that may be used by children to comply with children's privacy laws, which we aim to do as described here.

21. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version with a new version number and effective date. If a change is significant, we will also tell players in the game or in the store listing before it takes effect.
