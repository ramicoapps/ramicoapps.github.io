Shifts and pay

Keep track of your pay, easily

MishmeretShift tracker and pay calculator

Mishmeret counts your working hours for you. Tap "Start shift" when your workday begins, and it works out your hours, your gross pay and your estimated net pay.

At the end of the month, compare the calculated pay with your payslip and check that you were paid in full. You can manage several workplaces, log vacation, sick days and holidays, and get shift reminders.

  • Gross and estimated net pay against a monthly goal
  • Payslip comparison and report export
  • 7 languages, dark and light modes

Mishmeret calculates pay under Israeli rules and is available in Israel. The calculation is an estimate only. It is not a payslip, and it is not legal, tax or financial advice.

Cipher Worlds: MorseA game for learning Morse code

Pip sets out on a journey through ten worlds, and every level teaches more Morse code letters: listen to signals, decode messages and send your own.

Along the way you collect stars and items, build Pip's world and meet new friends. You can learn Hebrew Morse or International Morse.

  • Ten worlds with a story
  • Hebrew Morse and International Morse
  • In Hebrew and English
