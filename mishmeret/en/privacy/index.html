Privacy policy

Updated on 10.11.2026

This is an English translation. The Hebrew text is the binding version.

1. About this policy

The app is operated by ramicoapps, and we are responsible for this policy. It explains what information is kept and where, who receives information and why, and what your rights and choices are.

The app is distributed in Israel only, for employees who work in Israel, and is not offered in the European Union, the United Kingdom or the United States. The policy is subject to the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, and its regulations.

You agree to the policy on the welcome screen before the first use. It is part of the terms of use, and the Hebrew text is the binding one.

2. Definitions

"You" or "the user": whoever uses the app. "We" or "the operator": ramicoapps. "The app": "Mishmeret" for Android and iOS. "Personal information": information that identifies a person or makes it possible to identify them, including a device identifier.

3. Giving information and its purpose

There is no legal duty to give the app any information. Everything you enter is given voluntarily and kept on your device. Without a given detail, such as a birth date or credit points, the app still works, but the calculation that depends on it may be less accurate. The information is meant only to calculate and show you your hours, pay and rights, and is not passed to anyone. What the ad component and the version check receive from the device is set out below.

4. The information kept on your device

Shifts and absences, pay details and workplaces, extras, payslip data entered for comparison, imported payslips, tax details and settings (including reminders) are kept only on your device, in a local database. They are not sent to us or to the ad component.

On Android the app is excluded from Google's automatic backup, and the data is not copied to your Google account. But a direct transfer to a new Android phone (by cable or Wi-Fi) may move it, between your phones and not through us. On iOS the device's iCloud backup, if turned on, may include it. We have no access to these settings.

5. Permissions

All permissions are optional: without a permission, only the feature that needs it does not work. You can withdraw a permission at any time in the device settings.

Notifications: asked for only when you turn on a reminder (on Android 13 and above and on iOS), after a short explanation. They are created on the device and used only for the reminders you turned on, never for ads or marketing. There are no push notifications and no server that sends them.

Exact-time alarms (Android): so that a reminder to start or end a shift appears exactly on time.

Camera and gallery: with "Photograph a payslip", on Android the document scan of Google Play services opens and straightens the page. The scan runs on the device and the image is not sent, but the component may send Google technical usage data (such as device model and timing), and download itself from Google Play on first use. Where the scan is not available, the regular camera opens, and only then is camera permission requested, only to photograph the payslip. With "Pick an image from the gallery", the app receives only the image you chose, and not the rest of the gallery.

6. What is not collected, and how we use information

The app has no accounts or sign-up, no server, and no analytics or tracking of our own. So we receive no information about you or your use, and hold no database of what you entered. There is no automated decision-making about you or profiling of you by us. The information is used only by the app, on the device, to calculate the hours, pay and reports.

Version check: while it is active, when the app opens, at most once a day (and on every return to it while an update is required), it downloads a small file with its latest version from GitHub (a public hosting service owned by Microsoft). The request contains nothing you entered and no device identifier, but as with any network request, GitHub sees the device's IP address and technical details such as the operating system, and may keep them in its logs under its privacy policy. We do not receive this information.

7. Export, backup and payslip files

Through the device's share menu you can export a monthly report (PDF or Excel) and a backup file (JSON), and you can restore a backup from a file. The report contains the selected month. The backup contains all the data, including pay and tax details, and when the "Include payslips" switch is on (it is off by default) also the saved payslips, which include an ID number and a bank account. The files are not encrypted, and whoever receives them can read them.

You choose the destination. We have no control over the file after it is sent to another service (mail, cloud, messages), and there it is subject to that service's policy.

To share, the file is first saved in the app's private storage, and deleted from there on the next export or when the app is removed. When restoring, the copy of the file you chose is deleted right after reading, and the existing data is replaced only after confirmation.

You can import a payslip for comparison from a PDF file, a photo or the gallery. An imported payslip is read on the device only and stored in the app's private storage, and also in the backup file when the "Include payslips" switch is on. It is not sent anywhere. "Delete all payslips" in the ☰ menu, or removing the app, deletes it. A PDF is saved as chosen, and a photo is saved as one reduced copy. The temporary copy made when choosing is deleted right after reading.

8. Ads and sharing information with third parties

We do not sell, rent or pass on to any third party the information you entered. The ad component does not receive it either: the ad request includes only a flag for whether personalized ads may be shown. The ad component is the only third party that receives information from the device, apart from the version check.

The ads are third-party ads, today from Google AdMob, and full-screen only, with no banners. If other ad networks working through Google (mediation) are added, they will be listed here before they start working. Subscribers to "No ads" see no ads, and the ad component is not started.

An app-open ad can appear on the loading screen from the tenth opening, and from then on every second opening, but not when you return to the app while it is already open, or on a launch in which the consent form or the tracking request appeared. Other full-screen ads appear only: after "End shift" (a short ad and a long ad, which may include video, in turn), after adding an activity in the activity window, after sharing in "Export backup", and when closing every fifth edit of an existing shift. Except after "End shift" they are short (an image, without video). At least 3 minutes pass between two full-screen ads, even if you close the app and open it again. There are no ads in the middle of typing, in the first nine openings, or in the initial setup. You close them with the close button in the ad.

For this, the device keeps the number of openings, when the last full-screen ad was closed, how many ads appeared after "End shift" and how many times an edit of a shift was opened. This data does not leave the device and is not included in the backup.

The ad component uses the device’s advertising identifier (the Advertising ID on Android, and the IDFA on iOS if you allowed tracking) and technologies similar to cookies, that is, information stored on and read from the device, to fit ads to your interests, to measure their display and effectiveness, to limit how often they are shown, and to prevent fraud.

Google's ads component collects an advertising ID, an IP address (from which a general location can be estimated), interactions with the app and diagnostic data, for showing ads, measurement and fraud prevention. The data is transferred to Google encrypted (TLS), and some of it is collected even with non-personalized ads, for showing the ad and fraud prevention.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive it, only aggregate reports about the ads.

More information from Google: how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites ; turning personalized ads on or off in your Google account: https://myadcenter.google.com ; Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Consent and choices

In regions where Google requires it, a Google consent form (UMP) appears on the first launch, and the choice in it determines whether personalized ads are shown. Without the required consent they are not shown, and ads may not load at all. At the top of the privacy policy in the app (in the menu, "Legal", then "Privacy policy"), the "Ad privacy options" button reopens the form and lets you change the choice. The button appears only in regions where the form is required.

In regions where Google does not require a form (for example Israel), personalized ads are shown by default on Android. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the device settings.

On iOS the app requests Apple's tracking permission (ATT), after the Google consent step. In regions where a form is required, the request appears only if you agreed in the form to storing and reading information on the device. Personalized ads are shown only if you allowed tracking; otherwise, including if you were not asked, they are non-personalized. You can change this in iOS settings, under "Privacy & Security" and then "Tracking".

10. Buying a subscription

The purchase of and payment for an ad-free subscription are made with Google Play or the App Store, and your payment details are kept by them only, under their privacy policies. The app receives the subscription's details from the store (which plan, when it was bought and until when), keeps on the device only the plan, the end date and the time of the last check, in order to hide the ads, and does not send them to us.

11. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the content of your message and whatever you attached. Only this information reaches us directly. It is used only to answer and handle the request, never for mailings or advertising, and kept only as long as needed to handle and document it. You can ask for it to be deleted. We will pass it to anyone else only if the law requires it, for example under a court order or a demand of a competent authority, and only to the extent needed.

12. Transfer of information outside Israel

The information you entered does not leave the device. What the ad component collects is processed on Google’s servers, which may be outside Israel, in countries whose data protection laws differ, under Google’s responsibility and privacy policy. GitHub, which hosts the version file, is also outside Israel, and Google’s Gmail, where messages to us arrive, may keep them on servers outside Israel.

13. Keeping and deleting information

Removing the app deletes from the device all the data it stored, including saved payslips. "Delete all data" in the ☰ menu deletes, without removing the app, the workplaces, shifts, absences, tax details, comparisons, saved payslips, reminders and settings, and cannot be undone. An active subscription is not cancelled by it; you cancel it in the store. We have no copy, so the data cannot be recovered from us.

You can view and delete a saved payslip when you open it from "All payslips" in the ☰ menu, and all of them with "Delete all payslips" in the same menu. The numbers entered for comparison stay.

Files exported and saved outside the app (reports, backups) and the device's iCloud backup on iOS, if turned on, are not deleted by this, and must be deleted separately.

The data collected by the ad component is managed by Google. To limit it, on Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the device settings, and on iOS block tracking in iOS settings, under "Privacy & Security" and then "Tracking".

14. Security

The data is kept in the app's private storage and protected by the operating system's security mechanisms, so it is worth locking the device. The app adds no encryption of its own to the data and to exported files, so it is worth keeping a backup file in a protected place and not sharing it needlessly. The ad component's communication with Google is encrypted (TLS).

If a security incident occurs in the information that reaches us, that is, the email messages, we will act under the law, including notifying the Privacy Protection Authority and those affected when the law requires it.

15. Links to other websites

Links to the sources of the labor and tax rules, for example on the "Tax details" screen and the end-of-employment screen, open in the device’s browser. The app sends no data to these sites, and we have no control over their content or their policies.

16. Your rights

Under the Privacy Protection Law, a person has the right to review information about them held in a database, and to ask to correct or delete information that is not correct, complete, clear or up to date. The information you entered in the app is with you, not with us, so you review, correct and delete it yourself, in the app or by removing it.

For messages you sent us, you can ask to review, correct or delete them, at the address at the end of this document. For the information the ad component collected, contact Google through its tools.

You can take a copy of your data out at any time, as an Excel report or a backup file, and move it anywhere. You can also withdraw your agreement: change your choices about ads, as set out in the "Consent and choices" section above: in Israel, by resetting or deleting the advertising ID in the device settings (and on iPhone, by blocking tracking in the settings), and where Google requires a consent form, also with the "Ad privacy options" button at the top of the privacy policy in the app, or stop using the app and remove it.

If you believe your privacy rights were infringed, you may contact the Privacy Protection Authority at the Israeli Ministry of Justice.

17. Children and teenagers

The app is for employees in Israel aged 16 and over, including 16- and 17-year-olds, and is not directed at children under 16. Anyone under 18 uses it with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. The app does not ask for proof of age and does not verify it. It has no account, chat, public profile or sharing with other users.

We know the age only from a birth date in the tax details, which is optional. If entered, the calculation follows the rules for teens (for example the youth minimum wage). When it shows an age under 18, only non-personalized ads are shown, with a content rating suitable for teens (and if the rating cannot be limited, no ads are shown), and the birth date itself is not sent to Google. Without a birth date, ads are shown as for an adult and may be personalized, so anyone under 18 who wants only non-personalized ads should enter it.

A user under 18 must obtain the consent of a parent or legal guardian before buying a subscription; the app does not verify this consent. Parents can control purchases with Google Family Link (purchase approvals on Google Play) or Ask to Buy on Apple. If a minor bought a subscription without consent, you can contact the store or us, as set out in the "Refunds" section of the terms of use.

For parents: the data is on the teen’s phone, not with us, and all of it can be deleted with "Delete all data" in the ☰ menu or by removing the app. The calculation is an estimate only. For questions about teens’ rights at work (working hours, breaks, pay), contact the Ministry of Labor or "Kol-Zchut"; the app has links to the sources beside the items.

18. Changes to the policy

We may update the policy, for example when the app changes, when an ad network is added or when new requirements apply. The current version will appear in the app and on the ramicoapps website listed on the app's store page, with its update date at the top of the document, and the app will ask you to agree to it again before you continue.

19. Contact

You can contact us by email: ramicoapps@gmail.com
