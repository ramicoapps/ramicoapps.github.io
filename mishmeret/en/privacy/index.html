Privacy policy

Updated on 9.11.2026

This is an English translation. The Hebrew text is the binding version.

1. About this policy

Thank you for choosing the app "Mishmeret". We take your privacy and the protection of your information seriously. This policy explains what information is kept and where, who receives information and why, and what your rights and choices are.

The app is operated by ramicoapps, and we are responsible for this policy. The contact details are at the end of this document.

The app is distributed in the app stores in Israel only, and is intended for employees who work in Israel. This policy is therefore written under Israeli law. The app is not offered in the European Union, the United Kingdom or the United States, and is not directed at children under 13.

Before the first use you are asked to read the policy and agree to it on the welcome screen. The policy is subject to the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981, and its regulations. The Hebrew text is the binding one.

The policy is part of the app’s terms of use.

2. Definitions

"You" or "the user": whoever uses the app.

"We" or "the operator": ramicoapps, which develops and runs the app.

"The app": the app "Mishmeret" for Android and iOS, downloaded from the app stores.

"Personal information": information that identifies a person or through which a person can be identified, including a device identifier.

3. Giving information and its purpose

There is no legal duty to give the app any information. Everything you enter is given voluntarily, at your choice, and is kept on your device. If you leave out a detail, such as a birth date or credit points, the app keeps working, but the calculation that depends on it may be less accurate.

The only purpose of the information you enter is to calculate and show you your hours, pay and rights. It is not passed to anyone. Who receives information from the device, and what, is set out in the section on ads and in the section "What is not collected".

4. The information kept on your device

The app stores shifts and absences, pay details and workplaces, extras, payslip data entered for comparison, imported payslips, tax details and settings, including reminders.

All of this is stored only on your device, in a local database. The data is not sent to us or to the ads component.

On Android the app is excluded from Google's automatic backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account. However, when you move to a new Android phone and the system transfers data directly from the old phone (by cable or Wi-Fi), the app's data may move too. The transfer is between your phones, not through us. On iOS the device's iCloud backup, if turned on, may include the app's data too. This is a setting of your device, and we have no access to it.

5. Permissions

All permissions are optional. You can use the app without allowing any of them; then only the feature that needs the permission does not work. You can withdraw a permission at any time in the device settings.

Notifications: asked for only when you turn on a reminder (on Android 13 and above and on iOS), after a short explanation in the app. The notifications are created on the device itself and are used only for the reminders you turned on, never for ads or marketing. There are no push notifications and no server that sends them.

Exact-time alarms (Android): used so that a reminder to start or end a shift appears exactly at the set time.

When you choose "Photograph a payslip", on Android a document scan of Google Play services opens, which finds the edges of the page and straightens it. The scan runs on the device and the image is not sent, but the component may send Google technical usage data (such as device model and timing), and on first use it may download itself from Google Play. Where the scan is not available, the regular camera opens: only then is camera permission requested, and it is used only to photograph the payslip. When you choose "Pick an image from the gallery", the app receives only the image you chose, and does not read the rest of the gallery.

6. What is not collected, and how we use information

We do not use the information you entered for any purpose outside the app, because it never reaches us. It is used only by the app itself, on your device, to calculate and show the hours, the pay and the reports.

The app has no accounts or sign-up, and no server that receives data from it. It has no analytics or tracking of our own, and we receive no information about you or your use. We therefore hold no database of the information you enter in the app.

There is no automated decision-making about you or profiling of you by us in the app. The pay calculation is done on the device, for you only, and is not sent to anyone.

Google's component that shows ads communicates with Google's servers, as detailed in the section on ads.

Version check: while the version check is active, when the app opens, at most once a day (and on every return to the app while an update is required), it downloads a small file listing the app's latest version from a public hosting service (GitHub, owned by Microsoft). The request contains nothing you entered and no device identifier. As with any network request, the service sees the device's IP address and technical details such as the operating system, and may keep them in its logs under GitHub's privacy policy. We do not receive this information.

7. Export, backup and payslip files

You can export a monthly report (PDF or CSV) and export a backup file (JSON) through the device's share menu, and you can restore a backup from a file.

The report contains the data of the selected month. The backup file contains all the data, including pay details and tax details, and when the "Include payslips" switch is on also the saved payslips, which include an ID number and a bank account. The switch is off by default. The files are not encrypted, and whoever receives them can read them.

You choose where the file is sent. The app only opens the share menu, and has no control over what happens to the file after it is sent to another app or service, such as mail, cloud or messages. What happens to it there is subject to that service's policy.

To share a file, the app first saves it in its private storage. This copy is deleted on the next export or when the app is removed.

When restoring, the app reads a copy of the file you chose, deletes the copy right after reading, and replaces the existing data only after confirmation.

You can import a payslip for comparison: from a PDF file, from a photo or from an image in the gallery. An imported payslip is read on the device only and stored in the app's private storage, and also in the backup file when the "Include payslips" switch is on. It is not sent anywhere. "Delete all payslips" in the ☰ menu, or removing the app, deletes it. A PDF file is saved as chosen, and photos are saved as one reduced copy. The temporary copy made when choosing the file or image is deleted right after reading. You can view and delete a saved payslip when you open it from "All payslips" in the ☰ menu.

8. Ads and sharing information with third parties

We do not sell, rent or pass on to any third party the information you entered in the app. The only third party that receives information from the device is the ad component, as described here, apart from the version check described under "What is not collected".

The app shows third-party ads: today from Google AdMob, and only full-screen ads, in the places described here. There are no banners. If other ad networks working through Google (mediation) are added, they will be listed in this policy before they start working. Subscribers to "No ads" see no ads, and the ad component is not started.

The ad components use the device’s advertising identifier (the Advertising ID on Android, and the IDFA on iOS if you allowed tracking) and technologies similar to cookies, that is, information stored on and read from the device. They do so to fit ads to your interests, to measure their display and effectiveness, to limit how often they are shown, and to prevent fraud.

An app-open full-screen ad can appear when you open the app, on the loading screen: from the tenth opening, and from then on every second opening. It does not appear when you return to the app while it is already open, or on a launch in which the consent form or the tracking request appeared. Another full-screen ad can appear only in these places: after "End shift", a short ad and a long ad (which may include video) in turn; after adding an activity in the activity window; after sharing the backup file in "Export backup"; and when closing the window of every fifth edit of an existing shift. In all of these places except "End shift", the ad is short (an image, without video). At least 3 minutes pass between two full-screen ads, even if you close the app and open it again. There are no full-screen ads in the middle of typing, in the first nine openings of the app, or in the initial setup. You close them with the close button in the ad.

To decide when a full-screen ad may be shown, the app stores on the device how many times it was opened, when the last full-screen ad was closed, how many ads appeared after "End shift" and how many times an edit of a shift was opened. This data does not leave the device and is not included in the backup file.

Google's ads component collects an advertising ID, an IP address (from which a general location can be estimated), interactions with the app and diagnostic data, for showing ads, measurement and fraud prevention. The data is transferred to Google encrypted (TLS).

Even when non-personalized ads are shown, the component may collect some of this data, for showing the ad and fraud prevention.

Shifts, pay, tax details and any other data entered in the app are not sent to the ads component. The ad request includes only a flag for whether personalized ads may be shown.

Google processes the data the component collects, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive this data, only aggregate reports about the ads.

More information from Google: how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites ; turning personalized ads on or off in your Google account: https://myadcenter.google.com ; Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Consent and choices

In regions where Google requires it, a Google consent form (UMP) appears on the first launch. The choice in it determines whether personalized ads are shown. Without the consent that is required, personalized ads are not shown, and ads may not load at all.

On the "Privacy and ads" screen, opened from "Legal" in the menu, the "Ad privacy options" button reopens the form and lets you change the choice. The button appears only in regions where the form is required.

In regions where Google does not require a consent form (for example Israel), personalized ads are shown by default on Android. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the device settings.

On iOS the app requests Apple's tracking permission (ATT), after the Google consent step. In regions where a consent form is required, the request appears only if you agreed in the form to storing and reading information on the device. Personalized ads are shown only if you allowed tracking. If you declined, or were not asked, non-personalized ads are shown. You can change the decision in iOS settings, under "Privacy & Security" and then "Tracking".

10. Buying a subscription

If you buy an ad-free subscription, the purchase and the payment are made with Google Play or the App Store, and your payment details are kept by them only, under their privacy policies. The app receives the subscription's details from the store (which plan, when it was bought and until when), keeps on the device only the plan, the end date and the time of the last check, in order to hide the ads, and does not send them to us.

11. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the content of your message and whatever you chose to attach. This is the only information that reaches us directly. It is used only to answer you and handle the request, and we will not use it for mailings or advertising.

Messages are kept in our mailbox only as long as they are needed to handle and document the request, and you can ask for them to be deleted.

We will pass these messages to anyone else only if the law requires it, for example under a court order or a demand of a competent authority, and only to the extent needed.

12. Transfer of information outside Israel

The information you entered in the app does not leave the device. The information Google’s ad component collects is processed on Google’s servers, which may be outside Israel, in countries whose data protection laws differ. Google is responsible for that processing under its privacy policy. The service that hosts the version file (GitHub) is also outside Israel.

The email service where messages to us arrive (Google’s Gmail) may keep them on servers outside Israel.

13. Keeping and deleting information

Removing the app from the device deletes from it all the data the app stored, including saved payslips. We have no copy, so it cannot be recovered from us.

You can also delete saved payslips without removing the app: each one when you open it from "All payslips" in the ☰ menu, or all of them with "Delete all payslips" in the same menu. The numbers entered for comparison stay.

To delete all your data without removing the app, choose "Delete all data" in the ☰ menu. It deletes from the device the workplaces, shifts, absences, tax details, comparisons, saved payslips, reminders and settings, and cannot be undone. An active subscription is not cancelled by it; you cancel it in the store.

Files exported and saved outside the app, such as reports and backups, are not deleted on removal, and must be deleted separately. The same goes for the device's iCloud backup on iOS, if turned on.

The data collected by Google's ads component is managed by Google. To limit it, on Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the device settings, and on iOS you can block tracking in iOS settings, under "Privacy & Security" and then "Tracking".

14. Security

The data is stored in the app's private storage on the device, and protected by the operating system's security mechanisms. It is worth locking the device with a code, a password or biometric identification.

The app adds no encryption of its own to the data and to exported files. So it is worth keeping a backup file in a protected place and not sharing it with anyone who does not need it.

The ads component's communication with Google is encrypted (TLS).

If a security incident occurs in the information that reaches us, that is, the email messages, we will act under the law, including notifying the Privacy Protection Authority and those affected when the law requires it.

15. Links to other websites

The app has links to the sources of the labor and tax rules, beside the items they concern, for example on the "Tax details" screen and the end-of-employment screen. They open in the device’s browser. The app sends no data to these sites, and we have no control over their content. Once a page opens, it is subject to that site’s policy.

16. Your rights

Under the Privacy Protection Law, a person has the right to review information about them held in a database, and to ask to correct or delete information that is not correct, complete, clear or up to date.

The information you entered in the app is with you on the device, not with us, so you review, correct and delete it yourself, in the app or by removing it. We have no copy of it.

For messages you sent us, you can ask to review, correct or delete them, at the address at the end of this document. For the information the ad component collected, contact Google through its tools, as described in the section on ads.

You can take a copy of your data out of the app at any time, as a CSV report or a backup file, and move it anywhere. You can also withdraw your agreement: change your choices about ads, as described in the section "Consent and choices", or stop using the app and remove it.

If you believe your privacy rights were infringed, you may contact the Privacy Protection Authority at the Israeli Ministry of Justice.

17. Children and teenagers

The app is intended for employees aged 16 and over, and is not directed at children. Anyone under 18 should use it with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. More information for parents and teens is in the "Minors and parents" document on the "Legal" screen.

When the birth date in the tax details shows an age under 18, only non-personalized ads are shown, with a content rating suitable for teens. The birth date itself is not sent to Google.

We do not know the age of the person using the app unless a birth date was entered in the tax details. The birth date is optional. When it was not entered, ads are shown as for an adult, so they may be personalized. Anyone under 18 who wants only non-personalized ads should enter their birth date in the tax details.

18. Changes to the policy

We may update the policy, for example when the app changes, when an ad network is added or when new requirements apply.

The current version will appear in the app and on the ramicoapps website listed on the app's store page, and its update date is shown at the top of the document. When the policy is updated, the app will ask you to agree to it again before you continue.

19. Contact

You can contact us by email: ramicoapps@gmail.com
