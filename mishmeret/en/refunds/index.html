Cancellations and refunds

Updated on 8.11.2026

This is an English translation. The Hebrew text is the binding version.

1. What this document covers

The app "Mishmeret" is free. Its only purchase is the "Ad-free" subscription, which removes the ads and changes no other feature. There are no payments outside the stores and no free trial.

This document explains how the subscription renews, how to cancel it and how to ask for a refund. It is part of the terms of use.

2. Plans and billing

There are two plans: monthly and yearly. The final price and the period are shown on the subscription screen and in the store’s confirmation window before the purchase, and you are charged only after you confirm in the store.

The purchase and the billing are done through Google Play or the App Store, with the payment method saved in your store account, under the store’s terms. We do not receive or keep payment details.

The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period, at the same price, until you cancel it. If the price changes, the store will tell you under its rules, and where it requires it, will ask for your consent.

3. How to cancel

You can cancel at any time in the store’s subscription settings: on Google Play, "Payments & subscriptions" then "Subscriptions"; on iPhone, "Settings", your name, then "Subscriptions". In the app, the "Manage subscription in the store" button on the subscription screen opens that screen.

Deleting the app, or "Delete all data" inside it, does not cancel the subscription.

After you cancel, the subscription stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and does not renew. From the end of the period the ads are shown again. Your data in the app is not affected.

4. Refunds

On Google Play: you can ask Google for a refund within 48 hours of the purchase, on the order history page of your Google Play account. After 48 hours you can contact us at the address at the end of this document, with the order number (it starts with GPA), and we will review the request. An approved refund is made through Google, to the payment method you used.

On the App Store: refund requests are handled by Apple only, at reportaproblem.apple.com or in the App Store app. We cannot refund a purchase made on the App Store.

When a subscription is cancelled and stays active to the end of the period, there is usually no refund for the rest of that period, unless the store or the law says otherwise.

5. Your rights under the law

Nothing in this document limits a right you have under the Israeli Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981, and its regulations, including on cancelling a distance transaction and an ongoing transaction, as far as they apply to the purchase. If you believe you are entitled to a cancellation or a refund under the law, contact us and we will handle the request under the law, together with the store.
