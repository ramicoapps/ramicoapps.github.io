Minors and parents

Updated on 8.11.2026

This is an English translation. The Hebrew text is the binding version.

1. Who the app is for

The app is for employees aged 16 and over who work in Israel, including teens aged 16 and 17. It is not intended for children and is not directed at anyone under 16.

Anyone under 18 should use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. The app does not ask for proof of age and does not verify it.

2. What information is kept

Everything entered in the app is kept on the phone only, and is not sent to us. The app has no account, chat, public profile or sharing with other users.

The birth date in the tax details is optional. When it is entered, the app calculates by the rules for teens (for example the youth minimum wage), and the ads are adjusted to the age, as set out below. The birth date itself is not sent to anyone.

3. Ads for teens

When the birth date shows an age under 18, only non-personalized ads are shown, with a content rating suitable for teens (T). If the rating cannot be limited, no ads are shown.

When no birth date was entered, the app does not know the age, and the ads are shown as for an adult. Teens are therefore advised to enter their birth date. An "Ad-free" subscription is also available.

4. Purchases

The only purchase is the "Ad-free" subscription, through the store. Parents can control purchases with the store’s tools: Google Family Link and purchase approvals on Google Play, or Ask to Buy on Apple.

A purchase by a minor is subject to the store’s rules and the law. If a minor bought a subscription without consent, you can contact the store or us, as set out in the "Cancellations and refunds" document.

5. For parents

The data is on the teen’s phone, not with us. All of it can be deleted with "Delete all data" in the ☰ menu, or by removing the app.

The app’s calculation is an estimate only. For questions about teens’ rights at work, such as working hours, breaks and pay, contact the Ministry of Labor or the "Kol-Zchut" website. Links to the sources appear in the app beside the items they concern.

You can contact us with any question, at the address at the end of this document.
