Accessibility statement

Updated on 9.11.2026

This is an English translation. The Hebrew text is the binding version.

1. Accessibility of the app

We consider it important that the app "Mishmeret" be accessible to people with disabilities, so that everyone can keep track of their own shifts and pay.

We work to improve the app’s usability and accessibility. This statement describes the adjustments that actually exist in the app, and is not a statement of full compliance with Israeli Standard 5568 (IS 5568).

The adjustments were checked in software tests and in a manual check on an Android phone with TalkBack, a keyboard and Accessibility Scanner (October 2026). Some screens have not yet been checked manually, and the statement will be updated after they are.

The app is operated by ramicoapps, which develops and runs it. The adaptations described here were made in the app itself, not with an add-on or an external accessibility tool.

2. What was adapted

Screen reader (TalkBack): buttons, fields and choice options have a name and a role. An error in a field is read together with the field, and important messages, such as the start of a shift, its end and a failed save, are announced.

Text size: the text grows with the device's font size setting up to 200%. In languages with longer wording, the labels inside the vacation and sick-day bubbles on the main screen are fitted to the bubble and may be slightly smaller, up to a 130% font size. At larger sizes they are not shrunk. When there is not enough room, the screen scrolls.

Focus: when a dialog, sheet or message opens, focus moves to its title, and when a sheet closes it returns to the place it was opened from. The same applies at every step of the tour.

Color contrast at level AA in text and buttons, and a dark mode.

Buttons and touch areas of at least 48 dp, except the month bars in the activity chart (31 dp wide).

There is no rapid flashing, and the app respects the device setting for reducing motion.

3. Known limitations

A saved payslip that was photographed is an image, and a screen reader does not read its content. This is a technological limitation: the image is the user’s original payslip. The values imported from the payslip are shown as text on the comparison screen.

The monthly PDF report is not accessible to a screen reader: it is created without structure tags, because the device’s PDF tool that the app uses does not allow adding them. The same data is shown in the app itself and is available as a CSV file, and you can contact us to get it another way.

When the app's language differs from the device's language, the screen reader may read in the voice of the device's language. On Android 13 and above, you can choose a language for the app in the device settings.

4. Alternative ways

Each month's data can be exported as a CSV file and opened in any program that supports tables.

If information or a service in the app is not accessible to you, you can contact us and we will find an alternative way for you to receive it.

5. Ads

The ads in the app are shown by Google and are not under our control, so they may not be accessible. All the information about the service the app provides is available in it without the ads too.

6. Contact about accessibility

Have you come across an accessibility problem, or do you need information or a service in an accessible way? We will be glad to hear about it and fix it.

When contacting us, it helps to describe the problem, the screen where you came across it, the type of device and operating system, and which screen reader or assistive tool was used.

The business is run by one person, so no separate accessibility coordinator was appointed. Accessibility requests are handled directly by us, at the address below, which is also the app’s general contact address.

You can contact us by email: ramicoapps@gmail.com
